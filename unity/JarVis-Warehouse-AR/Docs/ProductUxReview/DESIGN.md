# Product/Label review surface

Operate; extends JarVis white–blue identity without replacing production design contract. Browser prototype only.

Palette: accent #1858d5, ink #223957, navy #142d52, secondary #52647c, line #d5dfed, ground #f2f6fb, surface white, selected #eaf1ff. Error #a32330, success #176349. Semantic colors also use copy, not just hue.
Font: existing BeVietnamPro-Regular.ttf self hosted. Scale 28/23 title, 19 section, 15 body, 12 hints. Spacing 8/12/16/18/20/24/28/32; 48px controls, 44px minimum. Radii 10 fields, 12 buttons, 16 panels. No decorative glass or fake barcode stripes.
Desktop: left nav 204px, flexible work area. <=1050 compact left nav and one-column confirmation. <=700 sticky three-item horizontal nav, single column form, wrapping category controls. Templates two columns mobile; collapsible families, native details/dialog/select/input.
Motion: only short hover response; asynchronous save/print state no fake business value animation; reduced-motion disables transition. Notice expires after 3.5s, action/page change clears it.
Native browser surfaces: accent caret/selection, scoped focus ring, skip link and thematic scrollbars. Keyboard and browser snapshots are not physical Samsung/Unity evidence.
Provenance: screenshot files are Playwright captures of local demo. No new raster artwork, no generated brand assets, no source upload.
